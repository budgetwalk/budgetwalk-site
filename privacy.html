The short version

We use personal information to deliver guide access, answer support requests, keep necessary business records and protect the service. BudgetWalk does not sell or rent personal information or use it for targeted advertising. We do not install advertising or audience-analytics tools on the BudgetWalk guide site. Third-party services, including checkout, have their own practices as described below.

Our legal bases

Where EU or UK data-protection law applies, we rely on:

  • Contract: processing necessary to deliver and support the guide licence requested by you.
  • Legitimate interests: administering purchases and support, securing the service, preventing fraud and licence abuse, and retaining proportionate records to resolve disputes, where these interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligation: processing required by laws applicable to us, where those laws qualify for this basis. Otherwise, necessary records for applicable accounting and legal requirements are kept on the relevant legitimate-interests basis.
  • Consent: optional marketing where you have agreed to receive it. You may withdraw that consent at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing.

BudgetWalk is based in Virginia, USA. We need purchase and authentication information to provide paid access. Support information is needed to handle your request. Optional marketing is not a condition of purchase. We do not use personal information to make solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

Your rights and choices

Depending on applicable law and the reason we use your information, you may have rights to access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests, including for reasons relating to your particular situation. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it. We do not send optional marketing unless you have agreed; withdrawal does not prevent necessary purchase or service messages.

Email us to exercise your rights. We may request proportionate information to verify your identity. We respond within the applicable legal deadline, normally one calendar month for EU/UK requests, and explain any lawful extension or refusal. We do not charge unless applicable law permits it.

A deletion request does not automatically cancel paid access. We will explain if deleting information essential to authentication would prevent us from continuing access and consider whether unnecessary information can be removed instead. We may retain records required by law or necessary for legal claims. For records Lemon Squeezy controls independently, contact it under its privacy notice; we can help direct your request.

You may complain directly to the relevant data-protection authority without first contacting us. UK users can contact the Information Commissioner’s Office. EU users can contact an authority in the member state where they live or work, or where the alleged infringement occurred.

Children

BudgetWalk is intended for adults arranging their own travel and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly seek personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action. Adults should supervise children using guides with them.

Security and changes

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the information we handle. No online service is completely secure; keep your licence key private except for permitted companion sharing.
